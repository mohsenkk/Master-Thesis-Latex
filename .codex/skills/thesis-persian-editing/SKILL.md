# Thesis Persian Editing

Use when drafting or revising scientific Persian prose in this repository. Follow the requested scope and structure; save completed sections in the authorized files.

- Arrange each concept as motivation and intuition, precise definition, then a useful equation, figure or example. Define symbols before interpreting an equation.
- Let chapter 1 establish the research question, scope, objectives and proposed contribution; place technical teaching and literature evidence in chapter 2. Cross-reference shared material instead of repeating it.
- Use consistent terms: میل اتصال، بازنمایی، رمزگذار/رمزگشا، مولد/تمایزگر، پیش‌آموزش، اعتبارسنجی. Keep DTA regression distinct from binary DTI and DDI.
- Cite the primary source for a concept or method; use reviews for taxonomy. Preserve the distinction between inspected article evidence, our interpretation and the proposed thesis method.
- Keep an unknown objective, convexity, novelty or performance claim as an open research item. Put precise [نیازمند منبع]، [نیازمند تعیین روش] or [نیازمند نتیجه آزمایش] markers at the relevant unresolved statement; consolidate repeated caveats.
- Use connected Persian paragraphs, نیم‌فاصله and consistent spelling. Introduce an English abbreviation once, then use it consistently. Make captions explain what the reader should learn and identify original republication versus an independent cited diagram.
- Preserve citation keys and mathematical meaning during language edits. Check labels, cross-references, figure paths and obvious LaTeX structure after revision; report compilation separately from static checks.
